Order of work

  1. Paste: tape the board in a frame of scrap boards, align the 0.10 mm stencil, one firm squeegee pass. Check the IMU pads: 14 clean, separate deposits each.
  2. Place top side with the list below, one value at a time. Do the BC15M and both IMUs first while the paste is fresh and nothing is in the way.
  3. Reflow on the hot plate (Sn63/Pb37: soak ~150 °C 60–90 s, peak 210–220 °C, then slide off to cool).
  4. Inspect the IMUs and BC15M edges with a loupe; fix bridges with flux + wick.

Reflow · hot plate, Sn63/Pb37 (melts at 183 °C)

  • Thermal camera: aim at the green solder mask beside the parts, emissivity ≈ 0.95. Shiny pads, solder and the plate read far too low.
  • Calibration for free: the paste goes shiny at exactly 183 °C. Compare with the camera reading and correct for the offset.
  • Limits: don't exceed ~230 °C on the board or ~90 s above 183 °C.
  • Carrier: set the board on a scrap of thin aluminium or a spare PCB, centred on the plate's hot spot, and move the carrier, not the board.
  • Dry run first: paste on a spare board, watch the camera, note the dial settings and times for your plate.
  • After: clean flux off with IPA, especially round the IMUs.
